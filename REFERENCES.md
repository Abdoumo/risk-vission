# Regulatory & Scientific References

AlgoRiskAI is designed in accordance with internationally recognized financial, regulatory, and academic standards, including:

*   **Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS)** – Basel II / Basel III frameworks for credit risk, market risk, operational risk, and capital adequacy (PD, LGD, EAD, Expected Loss)
*   **International Accounting Standards Board (IASB)** – IFRS 9 Financial Instruments, including Expected Credit Loss (ECL) methodology and forward-looking credit risk models
*   **Financial Action Task Force (FATF)** – global standards for Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF), used as a reference for fraud detection logic
*   **Bank for International Settlements (BIS)** – quantitative risk management principles and stress testing frameworks used by central banks
*   **European Central Bank (ECB) & Federal Reserve** – supervisory stress testing methodologies (CCAR, macroeconomic stress scenarios)
*   **Academic foundations in quantitative finance and machine learning**, including Value at Risk (VaR) models (Jorion framework) and explainable AI techniques such as SHAP (Shapley Additive Explanations)

These references ensure that AlgoRiskAI is aligned with globally accepted banking risk management and regulatory practices.
